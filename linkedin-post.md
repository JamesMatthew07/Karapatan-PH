_"There can be no tyrants where there are no slaves."_
— **José Rizal**, _El Filibusterismo_

I'm building a free web app to make sure no Filipino has to be the latter — at least not out of ignorance.

**The problem 🚨**

Too many Filipinos go through life unaware of the rights and protections the law already gives them. Not because we don't care — but because legal knowledge has been gatekept. Locked behind expensive lawyers, dense English-only documents, and a system that assumes everyone reads at paralegal level.

The result: people who could push back stay silent. People who deserve protection don't know they have it. And rights violations go unchallenged — not out of acceptance, but out of ignorance.

**Hello, I'm building KarapatanPH 🇵🇭**

A free web app focused on **basic human rights Filipinos encounter day-to-day** — not the entire legal code. Just the rights that matter when you're stopped at a checkpoint, fired without notice, denied a refund, or bullied by a barangay official.

No jargon. No paywall. No login. Translated into plain Filipino and English — the way a kuya or ate would explain it.

Inside:
→ 5 everyday-life modules — Police encounters, LTO/MMDA stops, Labor & employment, Barangay disputes, Consumer transactions
→ "What they CANNOT do" + "What YOU can do" — side by side, clear as day
→ Every right cited to the actual law (RA 7438, Labor Code, Rules of Court, etc.)
→ Emergency hotline directory: PNP 117, CHR, DOLE 1349, PAO 1343
→ Mobile-first, because most Filipinos browse on a phone

This isn't a replacement for a lawyer. It's the _first line of awareness_ — the kind that helps an ordinary person stay calm and know their footing when they're suddenly the one being questioned.

**The ask: I need lawyers ⚖️**

The hardest part isn't the code — it's making sure every right, every citation, every "what they cannot do" is **legally accurate**.

I'm looking for **Filipino lawyers, law students, or paralegals** willing to review and verify content before it goes live. Pro bono. Every verified card carries your credit (or stays anonymous if you prefer). The reach is potentially every Filipino who's ever been asked _"alam mo ba sino ako?"_ at a checkpoint.

If that's you — or you know someone — DM me or comment below. 🙏
